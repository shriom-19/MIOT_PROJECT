Electrical Safety and Hardware Protection Rules

  • Never connect a DC motor directly to an ESP8266 GPIO.
  • Never apply 5 V directly to an ESP8266 GPIO.
  • Select a motor supply compatible with the motor and L298N module.
  • Check motor voltage, stall current, driver limits, and power-supply polarity before powering the circuit.
  • Use a suitable current-limiting resistor with the external LED.
  • Ensure the ESP8266 and L298N share a common ground.
  • Disconnect power before changing wiring.
